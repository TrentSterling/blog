VibeSpace is a Three.js space-flight experiment with a procedural universe and a boosty autopilot. Fly through stars and nebulae, or let it take you around while you change the settings.

Generation work runs in a Web Worker, with active nebulae kept around the camera. Most of the experiment is in the movement and the layers of stuff passing you at different distances. There's no mission campaign to work through here, just space to fly around in.
