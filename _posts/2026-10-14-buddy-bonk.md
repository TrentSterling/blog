Buddy Bonk is a ragdoll sandbox you can play with up to four people. Grab a limb, swing Buddy around and let go. Right-click always does a quick grab, so you can move him or a prop without changing away from whatever toy you're using.

I gave the toys a few rules of their own. The mallet and frying pan respond to how fast you swing them. If you're playing with the basketball, just holding it inside the hoop won't count as making a shot.

The Toybox has tools, bodies, hats and items for the room. Everyone shares the room's cash, or you can use Sandbox to unlock the whole catalogue.

One player's browser runs the physics at 120 Hz and shares it with the others over WebRTC through Trystero. Here's a look at it.
