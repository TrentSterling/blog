FELLSWOOP is a floating island, a forest, and one very busy axe. Move the mouse over a tree and it gets chopped. The logs fly home, the stumps regrow, and you spend the wood on things that make the next day more ridiculous.

There are exploding pumpkins, trees that chain lightning, and beavers that gnaw through the forest and carry logs back for you. Move the axe off the island and it turns into a helicopter. You can also open Sandbox and try everything without touching your normal save.

The models, animation, sounds and interface are made in code, with Three.js embedded in the HTML. Progress stays in your browser. Come back for another day of chopping.
