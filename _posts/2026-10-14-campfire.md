Campfire is a little multiplayer place in the woods. Walk around as a procedural creature, sit by the fire, fish, skate, or put a hat on. You can chat with whoever is there, and there's optional spatial voice if you want to talk.

The creatures and their hats move together, and the skating stance stays attached to the board. Friends can join the same fire through a room link. Extra gamepads can join on the same screen too.

Movement and voice use WebRTC through Trystero. The world is Three.js/WebGL, while the chat, names and interface lettering are drawn with WebGPU. A lot of rendering for some little guys hanging around a fire.
