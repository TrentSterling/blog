## Archive notes (2026)

The surviving screenshots document several visual setups rather than one polished game trailer. An early editor capture has a square-headed character holding a blocky rifle in front of large green zombies with bright red eyes. Other shots pull back to a brown arena where tiny zombie figures gather around brightly lit characters. One frame puts several hat-wearing figures in the foreground with a wall of green enemies behind them.

There's more here than the original one-liner suggests. The images raise a neat design question: how small and simple can enemies, weapons, lighting, and crowd silhouettes get while remaining readable? They don't, by themselves, establish the controls, code architecture, or whether this particular build was ever released. For a separate, much newer voxel-horde experiment, see [Tiny Zombies: No One Left](https://tront.xyz/tinyzombies/).
