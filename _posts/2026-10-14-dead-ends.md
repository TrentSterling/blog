DEAD ENDS is a top-down zombie escape game. Four survivors, a lot of zombies, and a red safehouse door you're trying to get everybody through. Get the whole group inside and seal it. If you're playing alone, AI squadmates fill the empty slots.

There are five chapters now. You can shove zombies out of the way, revive a teammate or drag somebody who's down before the horde gets to them. The squad AI uses GOAP, with a director controlling the pressure as you move between safe rooms.

Online play uses PeerJS, with public rooms or a code to share with friends. Co-op is still experimental. There's also an optional native GPU renderer; the normal link keeps the Canvas version.
