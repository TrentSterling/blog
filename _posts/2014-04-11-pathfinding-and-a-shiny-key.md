## Archive notes (2026)

Most of the surviving detail is in the images. One is an isometric assembly of purple dungeon floor tiles, walls, pillars, and small stair pieces, with individual parts highlighted in the editor. Another is a much darker room: red highlights surround objects near gaps in the tiled floor, and a small yellow key stands out against the shadows. These captures preserve the look and layout experiments even though the post doesn't explain the pathfinding algorithm.

The contemporary note mentions Qubicle previews, YuukiRus learning 3D modeling, and a plan to combine voxel and conventional low-poly meshes. The images are a good snapshot of that crossover: a reusable tile-based environment with chunky props, lighting tests, and early dungeon presentation. Treat this as a visual development log for the Blocky Dungeon work, not a step-by-step pathfinding tutorial.
