Village is a god-game prototype where you've got a little settlement to look after and powers you can use directly in the world. There are three islands and six houses. Workers harvest and deliver food, quarry a finite supply of stone, and build a bridge to bring another recruit into the village.

Then there's a raid to deal with. Bless and Lightning let you get involved, along with grabbing things yourself. On desktop you can select a worker and set up a garden or quarry order before pressing Start Village, so the villagers don't get a head start while you're figuring out the controls.

Middle-drag or Shift-right-drag pans the camera, F focuses a worker, and Home gets you back to the starting view. AR and VR entry are there too.
