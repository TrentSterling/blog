Tiny Treads is my turn-based tank artillery game. You aim, pick a weapon and take a shot. The terrain is destructible, so the hill you're sitting on can look quite different by the time your next turn comes around.

There are diggers, jump jets and repairs in the arsenal too. You can fight the CPU, pass turns around on one screen, or play online. Quick join puts you in a public match, and a private code lets you get a game going with someone you know.

I also put in a garage where you can change your hull, paint and flag, along with your player name. The game fits into one HTML file, without external assets or anything to install. Online matches use WebRTC, with public MQTT brokers helping players find each other.
