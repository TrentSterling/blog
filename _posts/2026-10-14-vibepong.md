VibePong is two computer-controlled paddles playing Pong while the screen gets carried away with it. Trails, powerups and procedural effects all follow the match.

You can change the simulation speed, how far ahead the AI predicts and how much error it makes. Turn on the target display and you can see where each paddle thinks it needs to go. You're tuning and watching this one; both paddles are controlled by the CPU. It's built with Three.js.
