VibeMaze is a first-person maze drawn entirely in ASCII characters. You move around with WASD, look with the mouse and collect objects around the level. The map gives you walls to navigate, with collision to keep you from walking through them.

This was built with Gemini in vanilla JavaScript. The renderer includes the vector and matrix math, triangle drawing and depth buffer. It projects the scene's geometry, rasterizes the triangles, and turns the result into a new frame of characters.

The earlier ASCII Wolfenstein experiment used raycasting. This one has actual scene geometry going through that renderer, with no Three.js scene underneath it. Here's what it looks like running.
