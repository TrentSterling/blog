## Archive notes (2026)

The screenshots tell a much bigger story than the short original post. These experiments feature chunky, brightly colored aircraft flying over angular trees and simple terrain. Some frames show lines of red and white effects, bright impacts, and blocky debris. A later wide view includes yellow desert-like ground split by a turquoise river, with tiny aircraft and buildings in the scene. The changing environments suggest a series of visual and gameplay experiments rather than a single finished level.

The original note describes this as the last PlayMaker experiment before returning to C#. That makes the gallery a record of both a prototype and a workflow decision: use visual scripting to get ideas moving, then reassess how to build the next one. The screenshots show the art direction and effects, but they aren't evidence of a completed release.
