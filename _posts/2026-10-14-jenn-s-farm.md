Jenn's Farm is a little 3D farming game I made for the browser. Till some soil, plant seeds, water the crops, then harvest and sell them so you can buy more seeds. There are vegetables, herbs and flowers to grow, along with animals that produce things for the farm.

The market keeps track of supply and demand. If you keep selling loads of the same crop, its price drops. Leave it alone for a while and the price has time to recover.

I used a vertex shader to bend the world away from the camera, giving it that Animal Crossing-style rolling horizon. The textures are generated with Canvas and the sounds are made with Web Audio. It's built in Three.js, with click-to-play farming and an autosave in the browser.
