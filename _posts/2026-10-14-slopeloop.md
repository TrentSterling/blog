SLOPELOOP is a momentum runner inspired by HASTE. You've got 3.2 km of Sunfall Causeway to get through, and the idea is to keep carrying speed from one downhill landing into the next.

Collect burst charge, find a better line and try to beat your previous time. Rook gets a whole stretch of sunlit hills to run through. Here's what the course looks like in motion.
