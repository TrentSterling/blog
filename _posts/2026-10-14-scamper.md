I worked on the networking architecture for Scamper, a VR horror-adventure from Bezbro Games.

You play as a rat and use your hands and arms to move around, looking for another lost rat and helping it escape. You can play alone or with other players. The environments shift between different frequencies, taking you from bright natural spaces into darker sewers and city areas.

My part was primarily the networking. You can find the game and its current platform details on Steam.
