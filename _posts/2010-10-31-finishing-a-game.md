## Archive context (2026)

This is a small but revealing 2010 note. After reading a game-development article shared in FGL chat, Trent describes getting a second wind by cutting or simplifying his current project. The linked article, hand-drawn illustration, and embedded progress video were part of the original post and remain as historical references. Its proposed next-month release was a hope expressed at the time, not evidence that the game shipped on that schedule.

The project named in the original tags is Dynamite Max, from Trent's Flash-development years. The useful takeaway is the decision he recorded: reduce the size of the work until the end of the project feels reachable again.

### Looking back (editorial)

It's striking how early the finishing-versus-expanding problem appears in the archive. Four years later, the [12 Games in 12 Weeks challenge](/blog/posts/12-games-in-12-weeks/) tackles the same issue from another direction: impose a deadline and learn to handle packaging and distribution. These are two different attempts at the same practical goal, not two claims that a particular release succeeded.
