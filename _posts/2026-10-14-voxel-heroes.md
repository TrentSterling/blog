Voxel Heroes is a little voxel adventure that you can play with friends. Start in Mossbrook, get a sword and some equipment, then head out into the dungeons. You can split up too, so everyone doesn't have to follow one person around the whole time.

There are different eras to visit now. Fix something in the past and you can change the future garden. Your friends can be in another era when that happens. Mira and Tern can come along with the party, and being near them opens up combination moves that spend your magic gems.

It's built with Three.js. The characters, tools and rewards are all part of that blocky world, including the sword held over your head when you get it. Press L if you've forgotten what you were supposed to be doing; the journal keeps track.
