Aqua Empire starts with feeding fish and somehow ends up with a Broodship fight. Grow the aquarium, keep the shoal fed and protect the nursery. Beat the Broodship and you get a Crown Fish, then you can keep the whole empire going.

The fish show you when they're hungry: green when they're looking for food or prey, brown when they're getting close to starving. After the victory there's an endless mode with bigger waves and more Broodship visits.

It's all in one HTML file, including Three.js. You can export the aquarium as JSON and move it between the website and a local copy, so you don't have to abandon your fish when you switch.
