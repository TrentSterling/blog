A bunch of us devs decided to make life harder by compounding multiple layers of gamejams at one moment. Besides crunch from [OneGameAMonth](http://www.onegameamonth.com/) I now have to finish an RPG in 3 days. I'd like to thank [TheVillageBlacksmith](http://www.thevillageblacksmith.net/) for getting me involved in such a crazy plan. Worst case scenario, at least I have something for OneGameAMonth.

I don't know what rules were in place or if everything was supposed to be from scratch, but I'm the only one using Unity so I feel that I have to prove something to everyone.

## Archive notes (2026)

The three surviving screenshots show a miniature winter scene in a soft, tilted-camera style. A small character stands near a bright campfire, surrounded by wooden huts, fencing, rocks, and scattered props. Snow-like particles drift through the scene, while the camera's shallow-depth-of-field effect keeps the fire and character prominent. Even as an early jam build, it already has a distinct sense of place.

The original post makes the scope clear: this was a rapid RPG experiment, not a finished product. The screenshots capture the lighting, environment art, and presentation achieved during that short window. The original YouTube embed remains below as another piece of the archive.
