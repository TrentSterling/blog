BUTTLOADS 2 takes the old jam mining game into first person. You start digging under your backyard, sell what you find and upgrade the cutter so you can keep going down. Eventually that backyard hole turns into a shared mine, with oversized machinery to recover and something alive underneath it.

The original BUTTLOADS! was a 2015 jam game. This one has grown well past the early prototype shown in the gallery. There's a lot more mine down there now.
